Tips for Best Quality
  • Zero Ghosting: Standard (Right only) mode keeps 100% native sharpness on the left eye, completely eliminating warping on subtitles and lines.
  • 3D TVs (LG Cinema 3D / Samsung / Sony): Use Half-SBS format. On your TV remote, press 3D and select Side-by-Side.
  • Meta Quest / VR Headsets: Use Full-SBS for maximum sharpness or MV-HEVC for Apple Vision Pro.
  • Manual Retouching: Check Save Depth Map to edit tricky scenes in Photoshop/After Effects and re-export in seconds with Fast Re-export.
